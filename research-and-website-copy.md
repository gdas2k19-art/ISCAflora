# ISCA & Flora Play School
## Research, website copy and launch handover

**Research date:** 12 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata  
**Prepared from:** the supplied logo, supplied social profiles and Maps link, accessible public Facebook posts, the institution’s own YouTube channel, and a small number of corroborating search-index / directory references.  
**Status:** research-informed website preview and proposed copy; institutional approval is required before public launch.

## 1. Executive findings

There is enough genuine public information to build a useful, distinctive website without generic stock school photography. The strongest sources are the two supplied Facebook pages, specific public ISCA and Flora reels, the supplied Google Maps listing, and ISCA’s own YouTube channel.

The correct identity is **The Institute of Science, Commerce & Arts**, generally presented as **ISCA**, in **Topsia, Kolkata**. Some older artwork, including the supplied emblem, says “The Institute of Science & Commerce.” Use the expanded name appearing in the current Facebook / YouTube identity and Maps listing, while preserving the supplied original emblem. The acronym must not be confused with the unrelated Indian Science Congress Association or international organisations also called ISCA.

The institution publicly presents **2012** as its establishment year and uses the tagline **“Education with morality.”** This is a first-party claim, supported by its artwork and channel description, not an independently audited incorporation date. The YouTube channel’s identity, tagline, location and phone are publicly indexed. [1](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6LjSWLmtfEzQEOyso_-fPQ)

Its advertised educational pathways include Classes VII–XII, Science / Commerce / Arts, B.Com / B.A subject support, JEE / NEET enquiries and historical spoken English / computer / coding tracks. These must be described as tuition or academic support, not proof of a school’s board affiliation or authority to award university degrees.

Flora is the early-years part of the learning family described by the user. The public evidence corroborates the connection: both pages share the Topsia address and primary telephone, and ISCA’s Annual Function reel explicitly includes Flora children’s performance.

**Important limitations:** this is not a complete download of every social post or Maps photo. Instagram blocked public automated access; some Facebook photo detail pages required login; Maps showed a limited public view. No authentication wall was bypassed. No social-media access credentials were requested or used.

- Decision: use the accessible later first-party bands; add an eligibility confirmation note. The institution should approve the final age policy before launch.

### Fees

- Historical Facebook cover, referring to 2023–2024 achievers, advertises an “all courses” ₹6,499 yearly subscription.
- 2 October 2026 post advertises a one-year subscription offer: Science ₹4,000, Commerce ₹3,000, Arts ₹2,500.
- The exact meaning of “subscription,” course inclusions, tuition coverage, eligibility, validity and additional fees is not fully explained.
- Decision: no price table or “starting from” claim on the website. Present a direct fee-enquiry call to action. Preserve the offers only in this research record, dated and caveated.

Primary evidence: https://www.facebook.com/reel/3379914998855113/ and the ISCA Facebook public cover.

The March 2026 Flora reel says “Ramazan Offer: FREE ADMISSION.” This is a historical seasonal advertisement, not a valid current admission promise. The local reel is available only as a clearly labelled archive, with an explicit warning that the offer is not presented as current. Third-party music was removed from the local version.

### Facilities

The Flora cover advertises a fully air-conditioned classroom. The older indexed site describes outdoor play areas, gardens, library / reading nook, art studio and music room. These were not independently verified; the latter claims may be generic / outdated. They are not made on the live website. The available authentic classroom photograph is from ISCA’s Maps listing, not proof of Flora’s current interior.

### Awards, faculty counts and outcomes

Historical artwork includes 2023–2024 student percentages and an achievement collage. These are not used to create fabricated testimonials, a pass rate, a ranking or a “100% success” banner. Current 2026 photos show awards and certificate presentations, but are not an audited academic results table.

The Annual Function caption reports 270+ students and 20 faculty members at the event. These are event-specific first-party counts. They must not be converted into a verified current enrolment, current headcount or staffing ratio.

### Brand / legal status

A shared learning-family presentation is supported, but legal ownership, registration numbers, management names and formal organisational hierarchy have not been established. No invented founder or director is introduced.

## 9. Website implementation and what “dynamic” means here

The delivered site is React / Vite, with editable data separated in `src/data/siteContent.js`.

Working interactions:

- responsive desktop / mobile navigation;
- programme filters and detail dialogs;
- Flora stage tabs;
- image lightbox with source captions, previous / next controls and keyboard navigation;
- local video playback and click-to-load YouTube;
- FAQ accordions;
- click-to-load Google Maps, plus a correct external directions link;
- validated WhatsApp / email enquiry draft preparation;
- downloadable programme overview;
- source notes, privacy information, focus management, lazy image loading and reduced motion.

It is not a student portal, payment system, online exam platform, CMS, automated social-feed synchroniser or server-side admission application.

### Enquiry implementation

The form creates a prefilled WhatsApp message or mailto draft. The user must send it. No form details are saved to a backend or local storage, and the interface does not falsely display “enquiry submitted.” A prepared-message status gives an explicit fallback link if the external app does not open.

For a future server-side form, use an approved endpoint / CRM, spam controls, verified recipient, access controls, a retention policy and genuine delivery acknowledgement. Do not silently add storage of child information. The present form asks for an adult / learner name, phone and programme interest, not a child’s date of birth or identity documents.

### Privacy / external resources

Fonts, imagery and local clips are served with the site. The Google Maps frame and YouTube player load only after a visitor asks for them. Social links and WhatsApp open the relevant external platform. Third parties have their own terms and privacy practices.

### SEO

Proposed title: **ISCA Kolkata & Flora Play School | Education with Morality**

Proposed description: **Meet ISCA — The Institute of Science, Commerce & Arts — and Flora Play School in Topsia, Kolkata. Explore school learning, JEE / NEET enquiries and early-years programmes. Education with morality, since 2012.**

Use honest EducationalOrganization structured data, accurate name / address / phone, descriptive image text and the supplied profile links. No fabricated AggregateRating or accreditation metadata. Add the real domain, canonical URL, absolute social-preview image, robots.txt and sitemap after domain confirmation. Avoid ranking-oriented “best institute” claims without appropriate evidence.

Potential owner-approved search topics: ISCA Topsia Kolkata; Science Commerce Arts tuition Topsia; Classes VII–XII tuition Topsia; Flora Play School Topsia; Montessori enquiries Topsia Kolkata.
